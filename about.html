Behind the camera

A life near the water changes how you see.

Scott Simpson is a St. Augustine-based photographer and licensed captain whose work is shaped by years spent around boats, coastlines and remote places.

His photographs focus on scale, weather, texture and the brief moments when a landscape stops feeling familiar. The collection moves from Alaska’s raw edges to warm ocean light, working waterfronts and scenes encountered while traveling.

This site is deliberately simple: discover an image, choose the license that fits, then decide how and where it should live. Print buyers are free to work directly with their preferred lab instead of paying an inflated fulfillment markup.
